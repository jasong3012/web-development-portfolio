04 Agentic AI workflow

How I ship with AI agents.

Agents do the legwork.
I keep the judgment, the review and the accountability.

  1. 01

    Brief

    I turn business goals, brand rules and conversion targets into a clear spec and acceptance criteria.

  2. 02

    Plan

    An agent reads the codebase or CMS theme and proposes a plan, and I approve or redirect it before any code is written.

  3. 03

    Build

    Agents scaffold components, write custom code and refactor in small, reviewable commits.

  4. 04

    Verify

    I run accessibility, responsive, SEO and performance checks, then do a hands-on review of every diff.

  5. 05

    Ship & measure

    I deploy with analytics in place, read the data and feed what I learn into the next iteration.
